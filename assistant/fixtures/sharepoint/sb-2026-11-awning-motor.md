# Service Bulletin SB-2026-11: Awning motor AWN-1200 intermittent stop

Applies to: Aria, Summit and Trailhead units delivered between 2025-01-01 and 2026-06-30 with the powered awning option.

## Symptom

The awning stops part way through extension or retraction and resumes after the switch is released and pressed again. Owners may report a clicking sound from the motor housing before the stop.

## Cause

The limit switch harness AWN-1200-H1 routes over a sharp edge on the roller tube bracket. Vibration wears the insulation and the limit signal drops out for a moment.

## Correction

1. Disconnect the 12 V supply to the awning motor.
2. Remove the motor cover (four screws).
3. Replace the limit switch harness with part AWN-1200-H2, which adds a protective sleeve.
4. Route the new harness through the bracket grommet supplied with the kit.
5. Refit the motor cover. Torque the cover screws to 2.2 Nm.
6. Reconnect the supply and run the awning through two full cycles.

## Warranty

Labor allowance 0.6 hours under operation code AWN-11. Parts covered for units within the 36 month warranty window. Claims outside the window are not covered by this bulletin.
